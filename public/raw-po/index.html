Synopsis:

Wang Mo and her classmates are learning to dissect a frog in biology class. Outside on the playground, a group of boys is doing ah lu ba, a game of complicit aggression dressed up as celebration, on one of their own - Big Bear. The scalpel opens the frog at the same moment the boys close in on their friend. By the end of the lesson, Wang Mo's arm has broken into a severe allergic reaction, the frog lies cut wide open, and Big Bear is curled on the ground in pain. Three bodies that do not yet know what has been done to them.

Adapted from Wang Qin's novel Method of Cutting.
